EU Limit Context: The EU annual mean NO₂ limit is 40 µg/m³. Rome's 2024 annual mean (~28.4 µg/m³) stays below this threshold. However, 47 days exceeded the 40 µg/m³ daily threshold, clustered in winter months (Dec–Feb, Nov) when thermal inversions trap pollutants. Under new EU 2030 targets, the annual limit tightens to 20 µg/m³ — at which point Rome's current trajectory would fail compliance.

2030 EU target warning: The revised EU Air Quality Directive sets a 2030 annual mean target of 20 µg/m³. Rome's projected 2025 annual mean of ~27.1 µg/m³ remains 35% above this 2030 target. Without significant reductions in thermal energy production, traffic, and industrial emissions, compliance by 2030 will require structural interventions beyond seasonal variation.
2026 Forecast: Generated by extending the SARIMAX improved model with Italy's projected renewable energy growth (PNIEC targets: +8 GW solar, +6 GW wind by end-2026), and assuming current traffic trends continue.
